# CFD-Simulation-of-Flow-over-a-Circular-Cylinder-with-Elastic-Filament

Under the guidance of Prof. Y. Sudhakar, IIT Goa
## Overview
This project investigates the flow behavior around a circular cylinder using **COMSOL Multiphysics**, followed by the introduction of an elastic filament behind the cylinder to study its effect on the wake and drag.

## Work Performed

### 1. Flow over a Circular Cylinder

A 2D CFD model was developed to study flow over a circular cylinder at different **Reynolds numbers (Re = 20, 40, 100, and 200)**.

The simulations were used to:
* Analyze the flow behavior and wake development around the cylinder.
* Study the transition and characteristics of **vortex shedding** with increasing Reynolds number.
* Calculate and compare **drag coefficient (Cd), lift coefficient (Cl), and Strouhal number (St)** for the different Reynolds numbers.
* Visualize **velocity fields and vorticity contours** to examine the wake structure and periodic vortex shedding.

### 2. Elastic Filament Model
An **elastic filament** was then modelled to study its behavior under fluid flow and understand its interaction with the surrounding fluid.
The filament model was investigated for different flow conditions and Reynolds numbers to study its deformation and influence on the flow.

### 3. Cylinder–Filament Fluid-Structure Interaction
The elastic filament was subsequently introduced behind the circular cylinder to develop a **fluid-structure interaction (FSI)** model.
The combined model is being investigated to:
* Study the interaction between the cylinder wake and the elastic filament.
* Analyze changes in **flow characteristics, vortex shedding, and drag**.
* Compare the aerodynamic/hydrodynamic performance with the baseline cylinder.
* Investigate the potential of the elastic filament for **drag reduction** at different Reynolds numbers.
